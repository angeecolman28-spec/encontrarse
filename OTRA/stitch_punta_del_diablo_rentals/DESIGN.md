## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, tactile, and grounded aesthetic that bridges organic earth tones with crisp coastal clarity. Rooted in artisanal composure and natural balance, it marries contemporary editorial minimalism with warm, reassuring physical cues. The visual atmosphere rejects sterile tech neutrals in favor of earthy authenticity, quiet luxury, and deliberate breathing room.

The emotional tone balances confidence, wellness, and reliability. It speaks to discerning audiences who seek clarity without clinical coldness—lifestyle platforms, architecture & sustainable living brands, boutique travel, or modern human-centered editorial experiences.

Key styling principles:
- **Warm Minimalist Architecture:** Content structured with deliberate negative space, natural linen grounds, and purposeful typography hierarchy.
- **Organic Earth Contrast:** Warm terracotta-clay accents anchor interactive anchors, while mid-Atlantic sea tones deliver decisive visual direction.
- **Tactile Softness:** Subdued borders, delicate tone-on-tone cards, and layered earth surfaces replacing heavy artificial drop shadows.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly governed by the exact four extracted tones, establishing a balanced atmospheric hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#A67C52` - Clay Terracotta):** The dominant action color. Applied to primary call-to-actions, active indicators, brand marks, and high-emphasis anchors.
- **Secondary (`#4C7FA8` - Atlantic Blue):** The stabilizing structural companion. Serves interactive secondary elements, links, focus rings, badges, and contextual focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#889D88` - Sage Moss):** The grounding organic accent. Reserved for affirmative statuses, tags, environmental badges, subtle card accents, and secondary progress indicators.
- **Neutral / Surface Base (`#E3DDD2` - Linen Sand):** The enveloping foundational background. Replaces harsh pure white canvases to infuse organic depth and warmth across containers, borders, and ambient structural wrappers.

### Contrast & Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#E3DDD2` delivers a tactile, warm foundation that softens high screen fatigue.
- **Text Hierarchies:** Deep umber/charcoal tones derived directly from the warm clay scale ensure WCAG AAA accessibility against `#E3DDD2`.
- **Containers & Surfaces:** Subtle shifts in opacity of `#E3DDD2` combined with crisp, ultra-thin borders provide structure without needing synthetic grey tiers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, expressive character of **Epilogue** for display headings with the legible, friendly geometric precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interface labels.

- **Epilogue (Headlines & Display):** Infuses an editorial, artisanal presence. Its distinctive terminals and grounded baseline anchor the brand's tactile identity.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Labels):** Provides crisp clarity across long-form reading, dense card layouts, inputs, and button metadata while echoing the warm, approachable roundedness of the visual palette.

Letter spacing on large display titles is tightened to create focused visual weight, while label tiers apply positive tracking to maintain absolute legibility at smaller scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is founded on a fluid 12-column grid designed to adapt seamlessly across responsive breakpoints:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers center comfortably at a maximum width of 1280px to protect line lengths and atmospheric balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` gutters.

Vertical rhythm relies on multiples of an 8px baseline (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Card interiors prioritize breathing room, applying `space-lg` to separate content blocks, ensuring the tactile linen ground `#E3DDD2` acts as active structural separation rather than passive empty canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal layering and warm ambient diffusion**, deliberately avoiding cold grey shadows or stark elevated planes:

- **Surface Tiers:** Depth is established by placing elevated surfaces (crisp tinted whites and warm sand overlays) directly on the foundational `#E3DDD2` canvas.
- **Ambient Terracotta & Slate Shadows:** When elevation is required for interactive drawers, active modals, and popovers, shadows use a 4% to 8% opacity wash tinted with `#A67C52` or `#4C7FA8` with expansive diffusion (`blur: 24px` to `36px`, `y-offset: 8px`), creating a natural ambient glow like sunlight hitting natural textures.
- **Tactile Outlines:** Cards and surface elements feature a 1px border colored with subtle 20-30% alpha variants of `#889D88` or darker sand hues to preserve edge definition without harsh line work.

## Shapes

A roundedness scale level of **2 (Rounded)** mirrors the smooth circular harmony of the source palette:
- **Base Components:** Buttons, form inputs, chips, and badge elements use `0.5rem` (`rounded`) corners, balancing geometric discipline with soft touchability.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Container panels and dialog cards scale to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), softening the overall UI perimeter.
- **Hero Containers & Floating Modules:** Outer wrappers and feature banners expand to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), completing the calm, organic visual rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#A67C52`, text in high-contrast warm cream/white. Subtle hover shift darkening by 8% with smooth spring transition.
- **Secondary:** Bordered in `#4C7FA8` (1.5px solid) with text in `#4C7FA8` and transparent or 5% tinted fill. Solid `#4C7FA8` background on focus or active press.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text in `#A67C52` or `#4C7FA8` with a gentle 10% `#889D88` background wash upon hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Chips:** Pill forms (`rounded-full`) utilizing `#889D88` at 15% opacity for backgrounds, bordered with 1px solid `#889D88`, featuring text set in `#889D88` (or darkened for legibility).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Active state fills with `#4C7FA8` with crisp contrasting white text; inactive state features `#E3DDD2` with subtle sand borders.

### Input Fields
- **Default State:** Background filled with a clean linen wash over `#E3DDD2`, bordered by 1px solid `#889D88` at 40% opacity. Label typography in `label-md`.
- **Focus State:** 2px crisp border in `#4C7FA8` with a soft 3px outer glow tinted with `#4C7FA8` at 15% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Unchecked:** Inset border in `#889D88` over a light sand surface.
- **Checked:** Solid `#A67C52` background featuring a clean cream checkmark icon. Smooth scale micro-animation (0.9 to 1.0) on trigger.

### Cards
- **Structural Treatment:** Framed against `#E3DDD2` canvas with a lighter warm-tinted surface, bounded by a 1px perimeter in `#E3DDD2` darkened or `#889D88` at 20%.
- **Actionable Cards:** On hover, cards subtly elevate with an ambient 12px terracotta-tinted glow and a 2px vertical lift, signaling intuitive interactivity without visual noise.
